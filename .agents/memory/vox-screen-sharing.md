---
name: Vox screen sharing expectation
description: Product intent and limits for Vox's screen-sharing feature.
---

Vox screen sharing is for asking questions about anything visible on the phone during a Gemini Live conversation. It is not a game-specific assistant or a feature that should tap through games.

The user selected continuous screen sharing as the desired behavior. Vox sends sampled still images about once per second, not a raw video stream.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked for screen understanding like Gemini Live after seeing the feature labeled and failing as “Game assist.”

**How to apply:** route screen sharing through the opt-in Gemini Live session and use non-game-specific wording. Explain the sampled-image cadence and that Google quota or billing may apply.
